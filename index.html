What details should I send for a quote?

Product name, required purity or grade, particle size or form, packing, quantity (one-time or monthly) and your delivery location. The more detail you share, the faster we can quote.

What is industrial salt used for?

Industrial salt (sodium chloride) is a raw material for caustic soda, chlorine, soda ash and many other chemicals. It is also used in textile dyeing, soaps and detergents, water softening and leather processing.

Why is gypsum used in cement?

Gypsum is added to cement clinker during grinding to control how fast the cement sets. It makes up roughly 4-5% of cement.

What is marine gypsum?

Marine gypsum is calcium sulphate recovered from salt pans as part of sea-salt production. Gujarat's coast is one of the main sources in India.

Can I get a different grade or packing?

Yes. Tell us your specification and we will confirm what we can supply.
