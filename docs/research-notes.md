# AAJ Corporation – Industry research notes (18 Nov 2026)

## Industrial salt
- Gujarat produced about **85% of India's salt** in 2021-22 (about 228 of 266 lakh tonnes). Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu each made about 6%. Source: Indian Bureau of Mines, Indian Minerals Yearbook 2022 – Salt.
- Industry uses more salt than households: about 118 lakh tonnes industrial requirement vs about 63 lakh tonnes edible (incl. cattle feed). Industries consumed about 109 lakh tonnes in 2021-22.
- **Biggest buyers:** chlor-alkali (caustic soda, chlorine, HCl), soda ash. Others: soap & detergent, general chemicals, dyes, water softening, textile, leather.
- About 80% of India's salt is sea salt made by solar evaporation.
- Quality standard for chemical-industry salt: BIS **IS 797:1982**. Buyers usually ask for NaCl %, moisture, insolubles, particle size.

## Gypsum
- Main users in India: **cement** (set retarder, about 4-5% of cement), fertiliser, plaster of Paris. Agriculture uses gypsum to condition alkaline soils.
- **Marine gypsum** is recovered from salt pans during salt production, mainly in Gujarat and Tamil Nadu. All marine gypsum goes to cement.
- Other types: mineral (mined), phospho-gypsum (by-product of phosphoric acid), FGD gypsum (power plants).
- Standards: IS 12654 (building gypsum, at least 60% CaSO4·2H2O), IS 10170 (by-product gypsum, at least 70%). Marine gypsum listings often show about 98% purity.
- Source: Indian Bureau of Mines, Indian Minerals Yearbook – Gypsum.

## Competitor / similar sites
- Most small Gujarat salt and gypsum traders sell only through B2B portals (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, GlobalLinker) with short listings: purity %, formula, uses.
- Bigger players like Agrocel show a simple product table with formula and CAS no., but few packing details or industries.
- **Opportunity for AAJ:** a clear site that explains uses by industry, tells buyers what specs to send, and makes WhatsApp/call easy.
